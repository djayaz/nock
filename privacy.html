NOCK

Privacy Policy · last updated 10 September 2026

Nock is a puzzle game published by Vartix. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no social features, and it asks you for nothing about yourself. This page describes exactly what the app does with data, and it is short because the app does very little.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter and no tracking of any kind belonging to us.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress in the app's own private storage on the device:

  • which level you last played, and which levels you have finished
  • your best score and fewest mistakes on each level
  • how many lives you hold and when the next one is due
  • whether sound is on

None of it leaves the device and none of it identifies you. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which is a Google service. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and process data including your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with the ads. We never see this data ourselves, and we cannot use it to identify you.

What Google does with it is governed by Google's own terms:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time it runs, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. Your answer decides whether the ads you see are personalised. You can change it at any time from the privacy options in the game.
  • Anywhere, Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops apps from receiving one at all.

Children

Nock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us and we will act on it.

Permissions

The app requests internet and network-state access, and the advertising ID permission. All of them exist to load ads; the game itself makes no network requests and works offline apart from the ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
